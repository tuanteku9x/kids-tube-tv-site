Using the App

Kids Tube TV helps parents choose which YouTube channels appear on the TV. Parents manage the channel list, keep their PIN secure, and review content from the channels they enable.

YouTube Content and Services

The app uses YouTube API Services and YouTube's embedded player. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Channel owners and uploaders control their content, so videos and their availability may change over time.

Sign-In and Data

Signing in to YouTube is optional. Parents can disconnect the account on the TV, revoke access through Google, and delete data from the TV by following the steps in the Privacy Policy.
