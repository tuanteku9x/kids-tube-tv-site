Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy applies to Kids Tube TV, an Android TV app developed by Tuanteku, and to this website.

Google and YouTube Data

If a parent chooses to sign in to YouTube, the app sends an authentication request directly to Google and requests the read-only youtube.readonly permission. Parents sign in on Google's website. The app does not receive their Google password.

The app reads the account's YouTube channel name to identify the connected account. It also reads the subscription list, including channel IDs, names, and images, so parents can choose which channels are available on the TV. When needed, the app uses the YouTube Data API to look up channel information or video lists. The app does not modify subscriptions, upload videos, or post comments on YouTube.

Data Storage and Sharing

The TV stores the parental PIN, the channel list and which channels are enabled, the connected account's channel name, access and refresh tokens, and the last sync time. The developer does not receive this data on a separate server, sell it, or use Google data for targeted advertising. The app sends authentication, channel lookup, and video playback requests to Google or YouTube to provide the features you request.

If a parent scans the QR code, the phone and TV exchange channel management information over the local network while the parent settings screen is open. This website has no forms or first-party analytics scripts. See the Google Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices.

Disconnecting Your Account and Deleting Data

  • In the app, go to Parents → Sign out to remove sign-in tokens, the account name, and imported channels from the TV. Channels added manually will remain.
  • To revoke access from your Google Account, open Third-party connections and remove access for Kids Tube TV.
  • To delete all local settings, go to TV Settings → Apps → Kids Tube TV → Clear data. Menu labels may vary by device.

Parents and Children

Sign-in and channel list setup are for parents. The app does not require children to create an account or enter their name, email address, or date of birth. Videos play through YouTube's embedded player. YouTube may process device, connection, and playback information under Google's privacy policy.

Google API Data Policy and Contact

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. For questions about the app's data practices, contact the developer through Tuanteku's GitHub profile.
