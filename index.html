Kids Tube TV

Choose which YouTube channels your children can watch on Android TV and Google TV. Manage the channel list in the PIN-protected Parents section.

Set up your channel list

  1. Open the app on your TV, go to Parents, and create a PIN.
  2. Add channels using a link or an @handle. You can also scan the QR code on the TV and add channels from a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. To import your subscriptions, select Sign in to YouTube and enter the code on the Google page shown on screen. The app requests read-only access to retrieve your subscriptions and your account’s YouTube channel name.
  4. Enable or disable individual channels in the Parents section. Children can choose videos from the channels you enable on the TV.

Signing in to YouTube is optional. Read the Kids Tube TV Privacy Policy to see what data the app uses and stores.
